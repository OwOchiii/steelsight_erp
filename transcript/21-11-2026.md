### Telegram: one decision first

The `telegram_chat_id` column will be nullable, as you said, and workers without Telegram simply get nothing. The catch is that the ERP on Render goes to sleep when idle, while gate check-ins are written by the detection app. Here's my plan:

- **Linking:** the worker's home page shows a "Link Telegram" button. It opens the bot with a one-time code, and Telegram calls the ERP back, which wakes it up anyway.
- **Check-in and check-out notices:** the detection app sends these itself, right after recording the visit, because it's always awake on site. The ERP sends them for manual entries and corrections. If the ERP sent everything, notices would wait until someone opens the ERP.
- **Monthly schedule:** a "Send schedule" button for a month on the timetable page sends each linked worker their own shifts. After that, a change to a worker's shift in that month sends them only the change. Sending on every save would spam workers while a manager is still building the month.

Do you want me to go ahead with this? The other option is to send everything from the ERP and accept the delays.

### The zone→team idea

I wouldn't replace individual ID with it. I would add it as a fallback.

- **What you'd lose:** the safety score, score notices, appeals and the monthly accounting report are all per worker. A violation that only says "someone from Team A" can't lower anyone's score or be appealed. That would break most of the safety score part of the SRS.
- **What you'd gain:** it never mislabels a worker, which a misread vest number can.

The ERP already has the data for a middle ground, because shifts say which worker is in which zone and when.

1. **Vest number read:** link the violation to that worker, as now.

3. **Always:** record the team, so HSE gets team-level counts and alerts even when no individual is known.

That gives you your suggester's reliability without losing the per-worker features. It needs a small change on each side. Tell me if you want it.
